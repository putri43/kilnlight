KILNLIGHT

Fight by lamplight while monsoon rain hides every footstep. A round-based tactical shooter in a blacked-out foundry. Aurora attacks, Umbra defends. Win a round by eliminating the other team, or by planting the spike at A · FURNACEB · COOLING YARD and holding it until it blows — or defusing it in time.

The foundry is dark: the only clear sightlines are the warm pools under the hanging lamps. In a lamp pool you can be seen from far away; in the dark you are hard to spot. The rain drums on the roof and masks your footsteps — but a gunshot rings out and gives you away. Earn money for kills and round wins, spend it between rounds. Accuracy beats speed: stand still, tap, and mind the recoil.
